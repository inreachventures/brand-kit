# Decisions

Every brand decision Brindlewick has made, with the alternatives and the reasons. The newest
entry is last.

## 2026-11-20 Brand documents in UK English

- **Stage:** 0 Intake
- **Decision:** All brand documents are written in English with UK spelling.
- **Alternatives considered:** Dutch alongside English for the Netherlands shops.
- **Why:** "The Dutch shops are fine with English for now." Whether to translate later is open.
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Words and looks ruled out from the start

- **Stage:** 0 Intake
- **Decision:** Never use "disruptive", startup purple, or anything that looks like a bank.
- **Alternatives considered:** none; Maya ruled these out before any options were shown.
- **Why:** Maya dislikes all three. She wants workshop materials instead: "steel, enamel paint,
  chalk on a blackboard. Warm, not twee."
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Claims never made in public

- **Stage:** 0 Intake
- **Decision:** Never say in public what an individual shop earns, or any distributor's margins.
- **Alternatives considered:** none.
- **Why:** Both are the shops' and distributors' business, not Brindlewick's to share.
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Current website copy and placeholder logo dropped

- **Stage:** 0 Intake
- **Decision:** The current homepage headline, signup email opening and sales one-liner are
  replaced, not edited. The placeholder logo made in a slide tool is not carried forward.
- **Alternatives considered:** keeping the placeholder until a designer is hired.
- **Why:** Maya on the copy: "I hate all three." The placeholder was "just our name in a default
  font".
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Positioning: built by a mechanic, compared with the paper diary

- **Stage:** 2 Positioning and audiences
- **Decision:** Lead with "shop software made by someone who's actually run a shop" (option A).
  In copy, compare Brindlewick with the paper diary and WhatsApp (from option B), not with other
  software. Parts ordering is a supporting point, not the lead.
- **Alternatives considered:** B, off the paper diary (fewer calls at the stand), as the lead;
  C, quote and order parts in one go.
- **Why:** Maya: "It's true and it's the thing I'd say across a counter. But most shops are on
  paper, so I want that in there too." On C: "Parts is Tom's baby but it's not why people sign up."
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Three audience profiles; investors left out

- **Stage:** 2 Positioning and audiences
- **Decision:** Profiles for shop owners (buyer and user), parts distributors and hires.
- **Alternatives considered:** an investor profile.
- **Why:** Maya: "Not investors, not now." Shops come first, hiring second.
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Price not published for now

- **Stage:** 2 Positioning and audiences
- **Decision:** Brand documents say "a monthly subscription per shop" and give no price.
- **Alternatives considered:** publishing the price.
- **Why:** Maya: "Price isn't decided for public use yet." Deferred, not rejected.
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Core message

- **Stage:** 3 Messaging and voice
- **Decision:** "Brindlewick is workshop software made by a mechanic. It takes the bookings, the
  'is it ready?' calls and the parts orders off the bench, so the paper diary can go."
- **Alternatives considered:** none drafted separately; it follows the chosen positioning.
- **Why:** It carries both halves of the position Maya chose: who made it, and what it replaces.
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Tagline: "Workshop software, made by a mechanic."

- **Stage:** 3 Messaging and voice
- **Decision:** The tagline is "Workshop software, made by a mechanic."
- **Alternatives considered:** "Booking and parts software for independent bike shops"; "Fewer
  phone calls mid-repair"; "The workshop diary that texts your customers"; "Made by someone who's
  actually run a shop"; "Built at the bench"; "Admin done before you shut up shop". Struck for
  clichés: "The all-in-one workshop platform", "Seamless bookings for bike shops".
- **Why:** Maya agreed with the recommendation that it says what Brindlewick is and the claim
  nobody else can make. She liked "The workshop diary that texts your customers" but "it leaves out
  parts", so it became a supporting message for shop owners.
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Voice: "From the bench", with two principles from "Across the counter"

- **Stage:** 3 Messaging and voice
- **Decision:** Five principles: name the part, not the category; plain, but warm; start at the
  bench; confident, never loud; explain the why, in a sentence.
- **Alternatives considered:** Direction 1, "From the bench", on its own (plainer, more reserved);
  Direction 2, "Across the counter", on its own (warmer, more conversational, light humour).
- **Why:** Maya: "One, with those two from two. Two on its own is a bit chummy. Nobody at a
  counter talks that much. 'Warm, not twee' is the line."
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 What can be said in public

- **Stage:** 3 Messaging and voice
- **Decision:** Public: 140 shops in the UK and Ireland, about 20 in the Netherlands, nine people,
  fourteen months old, Maya's six years running a shop in Leeds. Not yet: the price, named shops
  (each needs permission), the distributor count (public once Tom confirms it). Private only:
  order volumes, to distributors at most. Never: an individual shop's revenue, distributor
  margins.
- **Alternatives considered:** none; Maya ruled on each item.
- **Why:** Maya on order volumes: "private, and only to distributors, and probably not even then."
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Logo route: wordmark and tile now, designer brief for later

- **Stage:** 4 Visual identity
- **Decision:** Generate a wordmark in the heading font and a square tile now (route 2). Write a
  designer brief for a symbol, to commission when there is budget (route 3 later).
- **Alternatives considered:** keeping the placeholder logo (route 1); commissioning a symbol now.
- **Why:** Maya: "Two now. Three later, when there's budget. Yes, write the brief so it's ready."
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Palette: enamel green and signal orange on chalk white

- **Stage:** 4 Visual identity
- **Decision:** Primary #2B5A36, accent #E0662B, ink #1A2119, paper #F7F4EC, neutrals #ECE8DE,
  #CFC9BA, #6B675C, #45423B.
- **Alternatives considered:** A as first shown, with a cooler green #1F5240; B, blackboard slate
  #2C3331 with chalk yellow #E9C46A; C, toolbox red #A3322A with brass #C08A2B.
- **Why:** Maya chose A because it "looks like the old signs", then asked whether the green was
  "a bit bank-ish". The warmer green was shown next to it and she picked it: "That's the green."
  B's primary was too close to the text colour; C's red reads as an error in a product.
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Fonts: IBM Plex Serif, IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Mono

- **Stage:** 4 Visual identity
- **Decision:** Headings and the wordmark in IBM Plex Serif 700; body in IBM Plex Sans 400; part
  numbers in IBM Plex Mono 400. Type scale 1.25 from 16 px.
- **Alternatives considered:** Manrope 700 with Inter 400; DM Sans 700 and 400.
- **Why:** Maya: "The others look like every app." She added the mono "for part numbers", and
  accepted the recommended scale.
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 No AI-generated images

- **Stage:** 4 Visual identity
- **Decision:** Brindlewick does not use AI-generated images anywhere. Photos of shops are taken
  with each shop's permission, case by case.
- **Alternatives considered:** allowing them for illustrations or blog headers.
- **Why:** Maya: "If it isn't a real shop it shouldn't look like one."
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Channels: LinkedIn, the monthly email and the website; Instagram as a bonus

- **Stage:** 5 Communication strategy
- **Decision:** Owned channels are Maya's and Tom's LinkedIn, the monthly email to shops and the
  website. Instagram has no cadence: a shop story goes up when a shop sends a photo and agrees.
- **Alternatives considered:** Instagram as a fixed channel.
- **Why:** Instagram had no owner and no hours. Maya: "Fine, Instagram's a bonus."
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Committed minimum: two posts a month each, one email a month

- **Stage:** 5 Communication strategy
- **Decision:** At least two LinkedIn posts a month each from Maya and Tom, weekly when they can.
  The email goes on the first Tuesday of every month.
- **Alternatives considered:** a fixed weekly post each.
- **Why:** Maya called a weekly post realistic but agreed it would not survive a bad month: "Two a
  month minimum, weekly when we can." The email day is what the team does now.
- **Replaces:** nothing

## 2026-11-20 Approval rules

- **Stage:** 5 Communication strategy
- **Decision:** Nobody approves the founders' own posts. Anything naming or showing a shop needs
  that shop's agreement first. Tom reviews the monthly email and product web pages within a
  working day. Maya decides in a public crisis.
- **Alternatives considered:** none raised.
- **Why:** Maya: "Nobody approves our own posts. Anything naming a shop needs that shop's OK
  first."
- **Replaces:** nothing
